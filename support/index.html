Sculpted Fuel Support

Help for the Sculpted Fuel iPhone app.

Contact us

Email privacy@jacintocodes.com with any question, problem or request. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what happened. Never send your password.

Account

  • Confirm your email: after you create an account, enter the 8-digit code we email you. No email? Check your spam folder, then tap Resend code. A new code can be sent once a minute, and only the newest code works.
  • Forgot your password: on Sign In, tap Forgot password? and enter your email. Enter the 8-digit code from the email, then choose a new password.
  • Delete your account: Profile, then Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and everything you logged.

Premium

  • Premium is a one-time purchase, not a subscription. It unlocks barcode scanning, AI meal suggestions, 7- and 30-day reports, and CSV data export.
  • New phone or reinstall: sign in with the same account, then Profile, then Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought with.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.

Food search and barcodes

  • Results come from the USDA FoodData Central database and Open Food Facts. If a food or barcode is not found, you can enter it yourself.
  • Nutrition values are estimates. Check the label for exact numbers.
