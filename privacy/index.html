Sculpted Fuel Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

Sculpted Fuel is an iPhone app for logging meals, macros and supplements. This policy explains what data the app uses, where it goes, and how to delete it.

The short version
  • We use your data only to run the app for you.
  • We show no ads.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and data inside the app at any time.

Your account

To use the app you create an account with your email address, a password and a display name.

  • Accounts are run by Supabase Auth. Supabase is the service that hosts our database.
  • Your password is handled by Supabase Auth. We never see it or store it ourselves.
  • Your email is used to sign you in and to send account emails, such as a password reset code.
  • After you sign in, your session is stored in the iPhone Keychain on your phone.

What you log

The app stores what you enter so it can show your totals, history and reports. This is kept in our Supabase database and linked to your account:

  • Your profile: display name, goal (bulk, cut or maintain), and daily calorie, protein, carb and fat targets.
  • Your meals: the meal type and time, and each food's name, serving, calories, protein, carbs, fat and fiber. If you scanned a barcode, the barcode number is saved with the food.
  • Your supplements: name, dosage, how often and when you take them, and whether you took or skipped each one.

Database rules let your account read and change only your own rows.

Data stored on your phone

  • So the app can show today's totals, meals and supplement checklist when you are offline, it keeps a copy of them on your phone. This copy is encrypted (AES-256). The key is created on your phone and kept in the iPhone Keychain. The copy is cleared when you sign out.
  • If you allow AI meal suggestions, the app remembers that choice on your phone, so it does not ask again. It is removed when you delete the app.

Food search and barcode lookups

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the request goes through our server to public food databases:

  • Text searches are sent to the USDA FoodData Central database and to Open Food Facts.
  • Barcodes are sent to Open Food Facts.

Only the search words or the barcode number are sent to these databases. Your name, email and account ID are not. Our server keeps a count of your searches to limit how many you can make in a short time. Our server logs, kept by Supabase, can include search words.

The camera is used only to read barcodes while you scan. The app does not save or upload camera images.

Purchases

Premium is a one-time purchase made through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card details.

We use RevenueCat to manage purchases. RevenueCat receives your Sculpted Fuel account ID and your purchase details from Apple. When you use a premium feature that runs on our server, such as AI meal suggestions, our server sends your account ID to RevenueCat to confirm you have premium.

AI meal suggestions

Premium members can ask for meal ideas. These come from Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic.

  • Nothing is sent to Anthropic until you tap Allow on the in-app permission screen.
  • When you ask for ideas, our server sends Anthropic your goal (bulk, cut or maintain), the calories, protein, carbs and fat you have left today, and your dietary preferences if you have set any.
  • Your name, email and account ID are never sent to Anthropic.
  • Our server keeps a count of your requests to limit you to 20 a day.

Exporting your data

Premium members can export their meals as a CSV file. The file is made on your phone. It goes only where you choose to send or save it.

Ads, tracking and selling data

  • The app shows no ads.
  • The app contains no advertising or analytics tools.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or rent your data.

Who receives data

  • Supabase: hosts our database, accounts, server functions and server logs. Like most hosting services, it keeps standard request logs, such as times and IP addresses, for a limited time.
  • Resend: delivers account emails, such as sign-up confirmation and password reset codes. It receives your email address and the email content.
  • USDA FoodData Central and Open Food Facts: receive search words and barcode numbers only.
  • Apple and RevenueCat: handle purchases, as described above.
  • Anthropic: receives the AI suggestion data described above, only after you allow it.

Each of these companies has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app: Profile, then Delete Account. Deletion is permanent and takes effect right away. It removes:

  • your account, email and sign-in;
  • your profile, name, goals and targets;
  • every meal and food you logged;
  • your supplements and their history;
  • your search and AI request counters.

The app then signs you out and clears the offline copy on your phone.

Deleting your account does not cancel or erase records that Apple and RevenueCat keep about your purchase. To ask for your purchase record to be removed from RevenueCat, email privacy@jacintocodes.com.

Children

Sculpted Fuel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child under 13 gave us data, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email privacy@jacintocodes.com.
